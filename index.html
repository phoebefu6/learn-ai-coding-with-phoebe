Learn AI Coding with Phoebe

Six sessions, 45 minutes each, and one real feature you ship through all of them. One well-known study measured experienced developers as nineteen percent slower with AI while those developers believed they had been twenty percent faster. Another measured fifty-six percent faster. Both are honest, and the researchers behind the first have since published a partial walk-back of their own headline. This course teaches you to read that, and then to work accordingly.

The six sessions 💻 · one real feature, shipped across all of them

Bring something genuine from your own backlog, small enough to finish in six sessions. By the end it is written, reviewed, secured and merged, and you know which parts of that you should have done differently.

📐 Session 1 · easy

The honest baseline

The 19 percent slowdown, the 55.8 percent speedup, and how both are true. Plus METR's own 2026 walk-back, and five real coding tasks that compile and run in your browser.

▶ Start here 🧠 Session 2 · easy

Context is everything

The model only knows what is in front of it, and a long session quietly gets worse. What auto-compaction throws away, why the vendor tells you to clear between tasks, and what a context pack should contain.

what it sees 🔎 Session 3 · medium

The review problem

Almost-right is the expensive kind: 45 percent of developers name it their top frustration and 66 percent spend more time fixing it. A reusable checklist for reading a diff, and where duplication creeps in.

read the diff 🛡️ Session 4 · hardest

Security and supply chain

People using AI wrote less secure code and were more confident it was secure. 205,474 hallucinated package names. Four documented prompt-injection CVEs up to 9.8, where the attacker never touched your editor.

the heavy one 🛠️ Session 5 · hands-on

Build the thing

The four tools as they actually are today, including the billing model that changed, the signups that are paused, and the fact that Codex has meant three different things. Then build with a stated permission posture.

the real build 🚢 Session 6 · hands-on

Shipping safely

AI now helps throughput and still hurts delivery stability. The control system is the deliverable. What you should never hand over, and how to measure your own numbers instead of quoting somebody else's.

What is honest here 🧾
